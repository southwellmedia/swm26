## Design Context

### Users

Southwell Media serves small and medium businesses, founders, and agency partners with equal emphasis. Visitors need high-quality websites and applications for mobile and desktop, and a trusted person to build them with.

### Brand Personality

Personable, experienced, hands-on. Michael is central to the studio: twenty years in the digital world, direct client collaboration, and ongoing relationships after launch. Writing should sound like a person, without corporate jargon or inflated claims.

### Aesthetic Direction

Preserve the homepage's experiential, interactive direction and existing ink-and-ice palette, large typography, and motion system. The Studio page expresses this through Michael's portrait, personal writing, and purposeful interaction. The homepage is the user's established reference.

### Design Principles

- Make the personal working relationship tangible.
- Give direct clients and agency partners equal prominence.
- Lead with websites and applications; connect supporting capabilities to those outcomes.
- Let interactive craft carry the story while keeping information readable on mobile and with reduced motion.
- Use actual project examples and confirmed facts; do not invent results, testimonials, or commercial promises.
